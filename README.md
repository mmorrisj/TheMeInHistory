# The Me In History

A choose-your-own-adventure engine for historical fiction. You build a character —
traits, skills, hobbies, interests — or bring yourself, and get planted into a
real historical setting. A generative narrator weaves you into the period, and
you learn history by being invested in what happens to you inside it.

The design premise: **history is content, not model memory.** Curated, sourced,
git-versioned "Era Packs" constrain what the narrator may claim, and every
chapter ends with a Fact & Fiction ledger separating what happened from what was
invented on your behalf.

See [`docs/DESIGN.md`](docs/DESIGN.md) for the architecture and build plan.
